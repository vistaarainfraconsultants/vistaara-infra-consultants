Capabilities

Our Core Services

Six integrated practice areas covering the infrastructure lifecycle — from first feasibility study through to construction-stage advisory.

01

Feasibility & DPR Services

Pre-feasibility and techno-economic studies, Detailed Project Reports, traffic surveys and demand forecasting, pavement investigations, road safety audits, hydraulic studies, cost estimation and BOQ documentation.

02

Survey & Investigation

Topographic, LiDAR, DGPS and Total Station surveys feeding every alignment decision.

03

Highway Engineering & Geometric Design

Alignment, geometry, pavement and highway engineering solutions developed against current codes and site reality.

04

Structural Engineering

Structural design, foundations, bridges, rehabilitation and strengthening support.

05

Pavement Design, Estimation & BOQ Preparation

Pavement design for Flexible, Rigid and Composite pavements, along with quantity estimation, rate analysis and detailed BOQ preparation for efficient and cost-effective road infrastructure.

06

Project Management & Engineering services (IE & PMC)

IE & PMC services focused on quality, compliance, construction supervision and efficient project delivery.

07

Project Advisory & Construction Support

Pre-tender engineering, design validation, quality control and monitoring through construction.
